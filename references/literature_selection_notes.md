# Literature Selection Notes

- Ratcliff, Gomez, and McKoon (2004) is the primary protocol source. Experiments 1-6 define high-, low-, and very-low-frequency words, pronounceable pseudowords, keyboard word/nonword decisions, 750 ms error feedback, 150 ms ITI, 30-trial blocks, and a 350-2000 ms analysis range.
- Balota et al. (2007) supplies large-scale external validation for visual lexical decision accuracy and latency across tens of thousands of words and nonwords.
- Meyer and Schvaneveldt (1971) supplies historical construct provenance. Its paired-string semantic-priming manipulation is not imported into this single-string baseline.

Citation counts were checked on 2026-07-12. The four-block release is a documented compact adaptation of Ratcliff et al.'s much longer experiment; within-trial response and feedback semantics are preserved.
